📖 How to Play

🎯 Goal

Turn your starting balance of $10,000 into $1,000,000 through leverage trading!

📈 How to Trade

  • LONG - Bet that price will go UP. Profit when price rises.
  • SHORT - Bet that price will go DOWN. Profit when price falls.
  • Leverage - Multiplies your gains AND losses. 10x leverage = 10x profit or loss.
  • Add Position - You can add multiple positions in the same direction to average your entry price (물타기/DCA).

⚠️ Liquidation

If your loss reaches 100% of your position size, you get liquidated and lose your margin. Higher leverage = higher liquidation risk!

📊 Chart Indicators

Moving Averages (MA)

• MA7 (Orange) - Short-term trend (7 candles)

• MA25 (Cyan) - Medium-term trend (25 candles)

• MA99 (Purple) - Long-term trend (99 candles)

When short MA crosses above long MA = Bullish signal (Golden Cross)

When short MA crosses below long MA = Bearish signal (Death Cross)

Bollinger Bands (BB)

Yellow shaded area around the price. Shows volatility.

• Price near upper band = Possibly overbought

• Price near lower band = Possibly oversold

• Bands widening = Increasing volatility

• Bands narrowing = Decreasing volatility, breakout coming

RSI (Relative Strength Index)

Measures momentum on a scale of 0-100.

• Below 30 = Oversold (potential buy signal)

• Above 70 = Overbought (potential sell signal)

MACD

Shows trend momentum and direction.

• Positive = Bullish momentum

• Negative = Bearish momentum

📰 Market Events

Random events affect price movement:

  • 🐋 Whale activity (large buy/sell orders)
  • 📰 News events (positive/negative)
  • 📈📉 Technical signals
  • 💥 Liquidation cascades
  • ⚡ Momentum shifts

💡 Tips

  • Start with low leverage until you understand the market
  • Watch RSI for overbought/oversold conditions
  • Don't go all-in - manage your risk!
  • Use DCA (물타기) to average down in losing positions
  • Take profits when you're ahead
