📖 How to Play

  • ⌨️Use Arrow keys (or WASD) on desktop to slide all tiles in a direction.
  • 👆On mobile, swipe in any direction to move tiles.
  • 🔢When two tiles with the same number collide they merge into one.
  • 🏆Reach the 2048 tile to win — but keep going to max your score!
  • 💡Pro tip: try to keep your highest tile in a corner and build a chain from there.
