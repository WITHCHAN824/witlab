🧱 Brick Breaker

Move your mouse or touch the screen to control the paddle. Don't let the ball fall!

📖 How to Play

  • 🖱️Move the mouse or touch/drag on mobile to control the paddle.
  • ⚽Keep the ball bouncing off the paddle to destroy bricks.
  • 💎Colored bricks may need multiple hits — darker bricks are tougher.
  • ⬇️If the ball falls below the paddle, you lose a life. Three lives total!
  • 🔓Clear all bricks to advance to the next level. Speed increases each level!
