📖 How to Play

  • ⌨️Arrow keys or WASD to change direction.
  • 📱Swipe on mobile or use the on-screen D-pad.
  • 🍎Eat the red apple to grow longer and earn points.
  • 💀Avoid hitting the walls or your own tail.
  • ⭐Occasionally a golden star appears for bonus points — grab it fast!
